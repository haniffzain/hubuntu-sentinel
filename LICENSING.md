# Hubuntu Sentinel — Licensing Information

Hubuntu Sentinel is currently under active development. Development source is not distributed from this public repository at this stage.

## Planned licensing model

The project is being prepared around a **source-available dual-licensing model**:

- qualifying personal, educational, research and non-commercial use may be covered by a Community License;
- business, enterprise, OEM, managed-service, redistribution and other commercial use will require a separate Commercial License.

The exact license terms that apply to a future public release will be published together with that release.

## Important distinction

Until a stable public source release is made, this repository should not be interpreted as granting rights to private Hubuntu Sentinel source code merely because product information or promotional assets are visible here.

Third-party components, if included in a future release, will remain subject to their own licenses.

## Commercial enquiries

Commercial, enterprise, OEM, redistribution and source-code licensing terms may be offered separately when the product reaches an appropriate release milestone.
